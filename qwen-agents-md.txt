3. Contracts (these are real; read them from the files, they will not drift)
Job object produced by engine.build(cfg), keys the app consumes:


id, topic, subject, template, templateName, audience, tone, voice{...}, aspect,
music, captions, title, hook, totalSec, words, description, tags[],
thumbnail{text, art}, credits, warnings[],
scenes[] { i, beat, vo, caption, onText, motion, art, whyArt, clip, sec, words,
          chars, start, end, cues[{text, at}] }
cfg in: {topic, template, lengthSec, aspect, voice, tone, captions, music, duck}.

at in cues = seconds; the player in studio.js and the encoder in tools_build/driver.py both consume cues as-is — that is why captions stay in sync between preview and file.

Stage keys (brand.js pipeline, also tools_build and the render log):

script → boards → art → voice → captions → encode → publish.

Caption styles (studio.js:328 STYLES and :455 CAPS): blocks (Fraunces 800, karaoke window), karaoke, clean (mono bar), none. Encoder mirrors blocks only; adding a style means both maps plus vid.py:burn().

Beds (studio.js BEDS): chalk, drive, lofi (synth.py, deterministic, 30 s), ai (bed-ai.mp3, model-composed instrumental — verified unvoiced), none. Mixed at volume=0.85-0.9 with fade.

5. Traps already paid for (each one cost a debugging cycle here)
Sandbox resets delete /tmp and site-packages. This build had no ffmpeg and no fonts after a reset, and tools_build still pointed at /tmp/qwenwork. Fixed: env.py resolves everything relative to the project; fonts are vendored in assets/fonts/ (Fraunces, Caveat) with system fallbacks and an explicit substitution warning. If you move this project, re-run verify.py before trusting any prior claim.
/tmp is a tmpfs sized ~2 GB. Frame dumps filled it and the browser's renderer crashed with a bare "Page crashed". Frame scratch now lives in .render_work/ on the real volume. Keep it there.
Encode is CPU-bound and the box has 2 cores. driver.render(..., workers=4) is the signature, but job_master.py/job_promo.py pass workers=2; raising it here adds no throughput. A 953-frame master ≈ 34 s, a 1,152-frame promo ≈ 46 s. Size the real fleet from that.
Browser JS caching in the preview: cache-busting is ?v=N on every asset URL. Bump it after edits or you will debug a stale file. tools_build has no such problem; the site does.
Vision QA over-reads downscaled contact sheets and invents clipping. Trust the numeric safe-area audit (driver.audit, wired into verify.py) over an image model's opinion about layout. I chased three "phantom" defects before switching to measurement.
Text never comes from the image model. Everything readable in a frame is typeset by the encoder. Two of four AIGC motion clips were discarded precisely because drawn text melted and limbs morphed. If a task asks for "generated on-screen titles", that task is wrong.
Do not delete a frame directory while a worker pool is live. A cleanup step racing frame_at produced FileNotFoundError inside a third of the plan. audit() and render() now create their own dirs (os.makedirs(..., exist_ok=True)) and scratch is per-run.
MutationObserver recursion: the output panel once observed class changes and re-triggered itself until the tab crashed. Any new "auto-refresh" hook should dispatch explicitly (window.__onStep), not observe DOM churn.
Referenced-before-created assets. The site shipped 404s for assets/audio/bed.mp3 and assets/img/studio-shot.png long after they were removed or renamed, because the HTML still pointed at them and the browser cached the old JS. Order of operations: generate the asset, then reference it, then bump ?v=. A 404 in a demo is a broken image icon on a marketing page.
Music model needs a lyrics field even for instrumentals (minimax contract). An explicit [inst]-style workaround produced a genuine instrumental, verified by listening to it. Don't assume "no lyrics" means no vocals.
Refactors silently detached UI handlers. After rewriting the output stage, #go-publish.onclick was null — the button existed, looked enabled, and did nothing. verify.py cannot catch that (it never opens a browser). Any agent touching studio.js must re-run the click-through flow at the end of §4, not just verify.py.
